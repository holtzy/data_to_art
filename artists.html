Meet the Artists

Data to art would not be possible without the 12 stunning artists who agreed to share their work with us. We're deeply grateful to them and hope you'll find the one that matches your taste and style here.

Data To Art is a living project. Our goal is to feature one new artist every week. If you would like your work to be included, or if you know someone whose work deserves to be showcased here, feel free to reach out to us.
