Nadieh Bremer

A data visualization designer and data artist, working under Visual Cinnamon, who creates bespoke, creative and vibrant visualizations of data.

LinkedInHomepage

Nadieh Bremer is an award-winning data visualization designer and data artist. With a background as an astronomer and former data scientist, she ultimately found her true passion in the art of visualizing data.

A recipient of the “Best Individual” award at the Information is Beautiful Awards, and (co-)writer of Data Sketches and her new book CHART, Nadieh works with clients such as Google News Lab, Sony Music, UNICEF, The New York Times, Scientific American and UNESCO. She particularly loves exploring the intersection of science and pop culture.

The giant nest from Searching for Birds, Nadieh's favorite piece.

At the heart of her practice is the creation of bespoke visuals, uniquely crafted for each dataset. She tells stories from data in ways that are both unique and effective, favoring designs that first capture the eye before guiding the viewer into a deeper understanding of the underlying information.

Her work often transforms large, complex datasets into engaging visuals, frequently through vibrant color palettes.

The five pieces of Elemental Flows: Earth, Sun, Water, Digital and Air.

Nadieh programs all her visualizations with JavaScript and D3.js, using them to create both data-driven experiences and data art. For static pieces, she occasionally uses Affinity Designer for the final textual touches.
