Let's add your work to Data To Art

Here is what we need to include your work and spread the word about it.

1️⃣

Tell us who you are

This is the top of your artist page.

  • Your name, as you want it displayed
  • One sentence that sums up your work
  • A photo of you
  • Your city and country
  • Your website and social links
ExampleFlorent's artist page
2️⃣

Share your story

A short bio that introduces you and your practice.

  • 2 to 4 paragraphs: your background, the themes you explore, your tools
  • Your favorite piece: we'll feature it next to your photo
ExampleFlorent's bio
3️⃣

Pick 1 to 3 projects

Each project gets its own page. For each one, send:

  • Its title and date
  • One or two sentences to describe it
  • A few paragraphs on the idea, the data you used, and how you made it
  • A link to where it lives online, if there is one

✨ We're really open to format. Have a specific idea? A video, a schema, something interactive? Just let us know!

Examplethe Naturality project page
4️⃣

Add your images

This is what makes the pages beautiful.

  • 5 to 10 images per project: the full piece, details, process, prints
  • As large as you have them, without watermarks
Examplethe images in Naturality
5️⃣

Send it in one folder

Put everything in a single shared folder and email us the link.

  • Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer all work
  • Rough texts are fine: we polish them for you
Exampleall our artists

🎉 What happens next

We build your pages and send you a preview. Nothing goes live until you're happy with it.

Your work stays yours. We always credit you and link back to you, and we can change or remove anything whenever you ask.

Questions, or ready to send? Write to yan.holtz.data@gmail.com
